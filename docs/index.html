Telegram-inspired message bubbles and a simpler, personal sidebar for Slack on the web.

The installation link will be added here after the unlisted listing is approved. Source and manual-install instructions are available on GitHub.

Make Slack your own

  • Readable message bubbles with your messages aligned right.
  • Containers and one level of subcontainers, with icons and colors.
  • Local pins and unread-chat badges that respect muted conversations.
  • A switch back to the original Slack layout.

Your organization stays yours

Preferences are stored locally in your Chrome profile. No backend, telemetry, or developer access to your Slack messages. Read the full privacy policy.
