Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Paperplane 0.5.0

Your Slack data stays in your browser.

Paperplane customizes Slack’s web interface. It does not transmit Slack data to the developer or operate a backend, analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

What Paperplane handles

On Slack client pages, Paperplane uses rendered workspace and conversation IDs, channel and contact names, section names, unread and mute indicators, and current conversation information to display local navigation containers. It reads profile and message sender IDs, including IDs in rendered avatar URLs, and message grouping/timestamp metadata to align message bubbles. CSS changes the appearance of existing messages and attachments; the extension does not copy or store their bodies or files.

What is saved

Chrome’s local extension storage holds your layout preference, any manually selected member ID, and per-workspace container names, icons, colors, channel/contact shortcut names and IDs, nesting, pins, and collapsed state. Observed unread and mute information is kept temporarily in memory. Paperplane does not use Chrome Sync or transfer your organization to teammates or other devices.

How information is used and shared

This information is used only for the visible layout and organization features you use. Paperplane does not sell, share, or transmit it to the developer or third parties; it is not used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending. It does not read passwords, Slack session tokens, cookies, or general browsing history, and does not call Slack APIs. Navigating a channel uses Slack’s normal interface or URL; Slack continues to handle its own service traffic under its policies.

Permissions

The storage permission saves your preferences locally. Content scripts run only on https://app.slack.com/client/* to restyle that interface and read the rendered metadata needed for these features. The extension includes all executable code locally and loads no remote code.

Retention and control

You can edit or remove containers and shortcuts in Paperplane. Removing a shortcut does not leave or delete its Slack channel. Switching to Original Slack stops Paperplane’s layout and retains your preferences for later. Uninstalling Paperplane removes its Chrome extension storage; it does not delete Slack conversations. Keep your browser profile and device secure: Paperplane does not add separate encryption to Chrome’s local storage.

This website and support

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor connection data, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from the extension, which sends no Slack data to this website. If you email support, your email address and what you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending private messages, credentials, or screenshots containing confidential information.
